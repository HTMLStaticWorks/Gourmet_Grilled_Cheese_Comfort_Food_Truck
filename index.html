The Food Truck Story

"We believe comfort food should be unapologetically cheesy, buttery, and nostalgic."

Born from a deep love for childhood memories and culinary craftsmanship, our gourmet food truck brings together rustic European breadmaking and artisan American cheeses.

Every melt is pressed on our cast-iron flat top with sweet cream butter, melted slowly to create that iconic cheese pull, and paired with our 6-hour simmered tomato bisque.

The Sizzle Formula

Why Foodies Rave About Us

100% Artisan Farm Cheeses

Real Wisconsin aged cheddar, Swiss Gruyère, smoked Dutch Gouda, and fresh buffalo mozzarella melted without compromise.

Small-Batch Bakery Sourdough

Naturally fermented 36-hour sourdough and thick brioche baked daily by local artisan partners for the ultimate buttery crunch.

Roasted Tomato Bisque Dippers

Fire-roasted San Marzano tomatoes, fresh sweet basil, roasted garlic, and farm cream simmered slowly for the perfect sandwich dip.

Fresh Street Food Culture

Every order is sizzling hot, melted directly on the truck griddle, and served with friendly smiles at festivals, parks, and pop-ups.
