From Dream to Wheels

How We Built The Ultimate Melt Truck

It started back in 2018 when two culinary school roommates realized the world took fine dining too seriously and comfort food not seriously enough. Why shouldn't a grilled cheese receive the same obsessive craftsmanship as a Michelin-star reduction?

We bought a retired vintage yellow utility van, stripped it down to the frame, installed a roaring commercial flat-top griddle and a soup kettle, and hit the streets. Today, our food truck feeds thousands of smiling people every week across downtown squares, neighborhood breweries, and private wedding lawns.
